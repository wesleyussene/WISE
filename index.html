01 / THE WISE APPROACH

Twelve years running businesses in Mozambique.

Wise Group was founded in Maputo in 2014. Today it manages, creates and operates three companies: the Mozo family of corporate services, Seeff Mozambique in real estate, and Pluribus in specialist healthcare.

Each company runs on its own name, its own team and its own clients. Wise is the structure behind them — the shareholder that operates, not the investor that waits.

I.

One contract

Office, equipment, vehicles, company set-up and staff wellbeing under a single agreement and a single point of responsibility.

II.

Local content

Every Wise company is 100% Mozambican-registered. Every service you contract counts as local spend toward your compliance requirements.

III.

Mozambican-led

Female-founded in 2014 and led from Maputo since. Institutional presence through AMESI and CTA.

IV.

Connected value

Companies that already work together: shorter procurement cycles, faster onboarding, one invoice.
